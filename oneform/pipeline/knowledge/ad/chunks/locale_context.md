# Locale & Context Knowledge

## Why Locale Matters

The same search query can mean completely different things depending on the user's locale, language, and App Store region. Always consider the market context before rating.

## Example: "BBC"

| Locale | Likely Intent | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| UK (en_GB) | British Broadcasting Corporation | News, iPlayer, Sounds, Sport apps |
| Thailand (th_TH) | BBC (a Thai cosmetics brand) | Bangkok Beauty Company |
| India (en_IN) | Could be either BBC News or a local brand | Context clues needed |
| US (en_US) | British Broadcasting Corporation (international) | BBC News app, not iPlayer |

**Rating impact**:
- Query: "BBC" in UK -> App: "BBC iPlayer" = **Excellent**
- Query: "BBC" in UK -> App: "BBC Thai cosmetics" = **Bad** (wrong BBC)
- Query: "BBC" in Thailand -> App: "BBC cosmetics app" = **Excellent**
- Query: "BBC" in Thailand -> App: "BBC iPlayer" = **Bad** (wrong BBC, also unavailable)

## Abbreviation & Local Service Code Research

Many queries are abbreviations or numeric codes with locale-specific meanings. Always research before rating.

### Numeric Codes
- **12315** (China): National consumer complaint hotline. An app connecting to this service is Excellent; a random number-themed game is Bad.
- **110** (China/Japan): Police emergency number. A public safety app is relevant; a random app is not.
- **999** (UK): Emergency services number.
- **311** (US): Non-emergency municipal services.

### Abbreviations
- **IRCTC** (India): Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation. A train booking app is Excellent.
- **JR** (Japan): Japan Railways. A transit app is Excellent.
- **NHS** (UK): National Health Service. A healthcare app is Excellent.
- **DMV** (US): Department of Motor Vehicles.
- **CPF** (Brazil): Tax ID number. A government services app is relevant.

### Brand Names That Vary by Locale
- **Grab** (Southeast Asia): Ride-hailing and super-app (like Uber)
- **Paytm** (India): Mobile payments (like Venmo/Cash App)
- **Mercado Libre** (Latin America): E-commerce (like Amazon)
- **Kakao** (South Korea): Messaging and services platform
- **LINE** (Japan/Taiwan/Thailand): Messaging platform

## App Store Market Differences

### Availability
- Apps available in one region may not exist in another
- Regional apps may have locale-specific versions (e.g., "TikTok" vs "Douyin" in China)
- Government and banking apps are almost always locale-locked

### Popularity Context
- An app that is the #1 result in one market may be unknown in another
- Search behavior differs: users in some markets search by category, others by app name
- Local alternatives often dominate over global brands in certain markets

## Research Protocol

When you encounter an unfamiliar query:

1. **Check the locale** -- what country/language is this task in?
2. **Search for the term** in the context of that locale
3. **Verify abbreviations** -- they almost always have locale-specific meanings
4. **Check numeric codes** -- they often map to government services or hotlines
5. **Verify brand names** -- they may refer to different companies in different markets
6. **Document your findings** in the comment for that item

## Key Principle
Never assume a query means the same thing everywhere. A 30-second research investment prevents a rating error. When in doubt, research the locale-specific meaning before rating.
