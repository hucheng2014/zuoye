Categories
Harm categories have specific definitions for the type of content that qualifies for that harm categorization. Review the harm categories
thoroughly. Just because a topic mentions a topic under the harm category, does not mean it falls within that category.
For example, mention of a violent act does not automatically qualify as Violent. It needs to fit into the specific harm definition of Violent.

TIA

L

Topics falling into Medical, Financial, or Legal fields are considered "regulated content" or "high risk" domains, as they are often associated with
government oversight and laws. When evaluating these topics, approach with a high level of care. Advice seeking (prompt) with specific detailed
advice (response) without thorough fact-checking, poses harm.
