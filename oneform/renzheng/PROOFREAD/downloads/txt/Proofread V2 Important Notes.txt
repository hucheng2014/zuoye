Overview

Proofread V2 is different from the existing Proofread guidelines (V1).
For V2, you will only evaluate 2 dimensions - Correctness and Completeness - by
answering multiple Y/N questions, with the derived scores automatically computed and
displayed for you.

To succeed at this project
•
•
•

•

Make sure to follow the Guideline Learning Path suggested in Page 3.
You will first learn Minimal Edit Principles and other principles for navigating ambiguous
cases.
Pay special attention to 5. Preserve Expressivity, Colloquialisms, or Slangs under the
Minimal Edit Principles section. In this principle, we have called out rules regarding how to
handle SMS abbreviations and informal context (which is one major requirement that
distinguishes Proofread V2 from V1).
In the Appendix, you can find Word Lists that include examples of abbreviations that should
or should not be expanded by the Proofread feature.
