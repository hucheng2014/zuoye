[New] Variety
                         [New] Variety
      NAVIGATION BAR
                         Variety refers to the visual and conceptual differences between multiple images representing the same input prompt. The visual concept can be people,
                         living creatures, objects and even emojis...etc. A high score is awarded when each image presents a distinct and meaningful variation in composition,
INTRODUCTION             pose, backdrop and additional attributes...etc. Sometimes lower variety is expected if the input prompt goes descriptive and exclusive.
