Hello,

I attended the webinar on "Rewrite with Follow-ups," but unfortunately, I wasn't in a position to ask questions at the time. I'm taking the liberty of making up for it here :)

My doubts mainly concern potential shifts in meaning between the different output versions compared to the source text.

For instance, it’s not entirely clear to me whether Q1 covers strictly nouns, dates, numbers, and the like, or if it also includes less "tangible" parts of speech, such as logical connectors or various types of modifiers. Here is an example:

[Source text]: "Ci tengo a difendere Maria. Lei è una professionista impareggiabile, ha un curriculum di alto livello, delle competenze difficili da trovare in giro e non ha mai saltato un giorni di lavoro. Inoltre ieri non è venuta perché aveva la febbre".
[I really want to defend Maria. She is an unparalleled professional, has a high-level resume, skills that are hard to find around, and has never missed a days of work. Furthermore, yesterday she didn't come because she had a fever.]

[Rewrite]: "Ci tengo a difendere Maria. Lei è una professionista impareggiabile, ha un curriculum di alto livello, delle competenze difficili da trovare in giro e non ha mai saltato un giorni di lavoro. Inoltre ieri non è venuta anche se aveva la febbre"
[I really want to defend Maria. She is an unparalleled professional, has a high-level resume, skills that are hard to find around, and has never missed a days of work. Furthermore, yesterday she didn't come even though she had a fever.]

In a scenario like this, should we penalize the Q1 sub-dimension (since "because" becomes "even though" resulting in a substitution)? Or Q2, because the core message changes? (Though one could argue, for example, that the core message is "defending Maria," rather than explaining the implications of her having a fever). Or perhaps (most likely, I would say) Q3?

Essentially, my questions boil down to what qualifies as "factual" in Q1 (can a logical connection or an adjective be considered factual elements?) and what is meant by "core message/intent" in Q2: if a very long text undergoes a slight shift in meaning strictly within a brief aside—without altering either the overall meaning of the text or the author's intent—does that impact Q2?

One last question: using the example above, and assuming this is the second rewrite, should I penalize the question "Did the edits produce any unintended effects?"?
