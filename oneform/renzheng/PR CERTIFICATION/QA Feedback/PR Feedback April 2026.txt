Overview of Error Trends 
• The top error category was Localization, where graders frequently missed to mark 
localization issues present in the responses. Please remember all kinds of 
language/locale issues are considered Localization errors according to the v5 PR 
Guidelines (Preference Ranking_V5_Vendor 26.02.11) 
• Instruction Following and Truthfulness are frequently mixed up, resulting in an 
overpenalization of Instruction Following and underpenalizing of Truthfulness. 
• Truthfulness is also underpenalized because of lack of sufficient fact-checking, as well as 
a lack of understanding contextual correctness.

• Instruction Following vs Truthfulness 
1.  In cases where the model attempts to fulfil the user request and attempts to follow 
the instructions, but gives out incorrect information, this should be penalized in 
Truthfulness, not Instruction Following. In the below example (roughly translated 
from ar_ae), the activities provided include ice skating, which does not make sense 
as Al Dhaid is located in a desert region with quite hot temperatures.

this question, regardless of whether the answer is correct or not, you should not

Corrective actions 
- P lease ensure you are checking for ALL kinds of language/locale issues in the response,
since they are all considered Localization issues. This includes: all typos/spellings errors,
all grammar and punctuation errors, cases where just one word is ina. Different
language, etc.
- P lease re -check the Truthfulness section in the Guidelines, and focus on the two parts of
it: Factual Correctness and Contextual Correctness. Contextual Correctness means that
your new ‘truth’ is the input text provided by the user, where applicable of course. Ex: if
the user requested a summary of an input text, the summary must not introduce new
information/hallucinations, or include info that is untrue when compared to this input.
- P lease also remember that Truthfulness  is not applicable to fictional creative writing.
This type of content allows for imagination, made-up facts etc. Just be sure your case
really is fictional creative writing, since for non-fiction your response must be fact-
checked as usual.
- Remember that for Question & Answers user requests, if the response is attempting to
answer the question, it is following the instructions even if the answer is wrong. This
wrongness of the answer must be penalized in Truthfulness, not Instruction Following.
