## The Task: Rating the quality of transitions.

The audio clip is a crafted transition, i.e. a concatenation from two audio segments from the same song (the playback start with a first segment and then crossfade at the transition time into another segment).
Please evaluate the quality of the transition in the audio clip on a scale from `Awful` to `Excellent`.

Headphones or external speakers are required for this task to ensure appropriate audio quality (not built-in speakers).
